Daily Routine Checklist

Goal: Launch platform v1.0 on Product Hunt by October 1st — deadline: October 1st

Tip: print this page (Cmd+P) and choose "Save as PDF" for a daily paper copy.

Anchor Habits (When-Where Implementation Intentions)

  • I will ship one scoped commit to main at 8:00 AM in my home office desk
  • I will walk 20 minutes at 6:45 AM in the park loop by my house
  • I will post one build-in-public update at 5:00 PM in my desk, right after shutdown ritual
  • I will send 2 customized outreach emails at 11:00 AM in the kitchen table during email batch
  • I will write 3 journal lines about why this matters at 9:30 PM in the armchair in the bedroom
  • I will write tomorrow's top task on a sticky note at 5:30 PM in my monitor before closing the laptop

Weekly Actions (by day)

Monday

  • Review the sprint board and cut scope if behind
  • Review burn rate against the runway sheet
  • Review the failure-pattern list and pick one guard

Tuesday

  • Co-working session with accountability partner

Wednesday

  • Run one 20-minute user interview
  • Check subscription list and cancel one unused tool

Thursday

  • Draft one newsletter issue
  • Share one struggle honestly in the group

Friday

  • Review analytics and write 3 takeaways
  • Update the top-3 pain points list
  • Review the interrupt list and prune commitments
  • Thank one person who helped me this week

Saturday

  • One longer run or hike
  • Call one supportive friend or mentor

Sunday

  • Prep 3 healthy lunches for the week
  • Reconcile accounts

If-Then Emergency Scripts

  1. If I get stuck refactoring code for over 90 minutes, then I will close my IDE and write my daily marketing post immediately.
  2. If I skip the morning walk two days in a row, then I will put my running shoes next to the bed tonight and walk before checking any screen tomorrow.
  3. If a user gives harsh feedback and I feel like quitting, then I will log the feedback verbatim in the tracker, close the tab, and reread my Why statement before responding.
